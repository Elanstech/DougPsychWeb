Coaching or Therapy?

You're here

Coaching is the right fit if you're…

  • Functioning well, but ready for more
  • Growing your career or your business
  • Stepping up as a stronger leader
  • Facing a major life or career decision
  • Wanting a partner to keep you accountable

  • Healing from trauma or a painful past
  • Struggling with anxiety or depression
  • Working through deeper emotional challenges
  • Looking to feel like yourself again
  • Needing your health insurance to cover the costs of therapy

How We'll Work

The Self-Leadership Transformation Framework

A proven three-step process that gets you unstuck, clarifies your direction, and builds the mindset and habits to perform at your best.

01

Observe

We get clear on where you are and where you want to be — your goals, your strengths, and what's really standing in the way.

02

Assess

We map your internal operating system and surface the core beliefs, assumptions, and blind spots holding you back.

03

Address

We replace the habits, self-doubt, and old stories in your way — with mindfulness, cognitive reframing, and visualization — so you lead with confidence, focus, and follow-through.

About Rini

Rini Adhikari is an Executive and Leadership Coach, Facilitator and Trainer with over 20 years of corporate leadership experience across marketing, finance, digital strategy and consulting. She has held senior roles with Microsoft, J.P. Morgan and the Times of India Group, and brings a strong understanding of the realities, pressures and complexities senior leaders face.

An ICF Professional Certified Coach (PCC), Rini works with senior leaders, executives and C-suite clients across industries and geographies. Her coaching focuses on leadership presence, stakeholder management, navigating change, career transitions, team effectiveness and building greater self-awareness and emotional intelligence.

As a facilitator and trainer, Rini designs and delivers practical, engaging learning experiences in leadership development, emotional intelligence, communication, cross-cultural effectiveness, business storytelling, DEI and women’s leadership. Her sessions are highly interactive, with a strong focus on reflection, real-world application and behaviour change.

Having lived and worked across India, Hong Kong and the United States, Rini brings a strong cross-cultural lens to her work. Her approach is warm, practical and reflective, while also challenging leaders to look beyond immediate issues, examine the wider system they operate in, and identify the shifts that will create meaningful and sustainable impact.

Rini is certified in Hogan Assessments, Emotional Intelligence, Positive Intelligence, NLP, Transactional Analysis and Cognitive Behavioural Coaching (CBT). She is also an ICF Mentor Coach and Coach Performance Assessor. She serves on the Board of ICF New York and is committed to contributing to the development of the wider coaching profession.

Success stories

Global fintech

From managing the work to leading the team

A senior leader came into coaching while managing a rapidly growing team, which had expanded from 3 to 20 people. The challenge was to step back from doing and solving everything personally and become more intentional about delegation, visibility and development.

Through coaching, the leader began creating more opportunities for team members to take ownership of challenging work, gain exposure and learn from one another. They also became more conscious about recognising success and giving people greater freedom in how they delivered results. The shift was from being the person who carried the work to becoming the leader who enabled others to succeed.

Manufacturing & materials

Strengthening influence and executive presence

A senior technology and analytics leader was highly credible in their area of expertise but was finding it challenging to navigate peer dynamics, stakeholder resistance and difficult conversations at a more senior level.

Coaching focused on executive presence, stakeholder mapping and influencing without relying on authority. Over time, the leader became more confident in senior-level conversations, more deliberate in managing stakeholder relationships and better able to influence across the organisation without unnecessary escalation.

About Maxine

Maxine Attong spent more than 20 years inside organizations—as a finance executive, Vice President of Strategy and Chief Operating Officer—before building a practice focused on the people, teams and systems responsible for delivering results.

A Gestalt Organizational Leadership and Development practitioner, Maxine works with senior leaders and teams across North America, Europe, Africa, Latin America and the Caribbean. Her clients span financial services, healthcare, energy and other sectors, and include JMMB Bank, Children’s Hospital of The King’s Daughters, MJS Designs and United Nations agencies.

Her work encompasses executive and team coaching, leadership development, strategy execution, and the design and facilitation of leadership retreats and team-effectiveness interventions. Her engagements range from individual executive coaching to multi-month leadership programmes involving more than 200 managers.

Working at the intersection of strategy, people and change, Maxine helps leaders make the invisible visible—the underlying patterns that shape trust, decision-making, performance and culture. With more than 4,000 coaching hours and a CPA/CMA background, she combines commercial judgement and executive experience with deep insight into human and organizational behaviour.

Maxine is an ICF Professional Certified Coach (PCC), Certified Professional Facilitator (CPF), and Certified Gestalt Organizational Leadership and Development Practitioner. She holds an M.Sc. in Organizational Development (Gestalt, Leadership and Change) and a B.Sc. in Accounting.

She is the author of Lead Your Team to Win and Change or Die: The Business Process Improvement Manual. Her latest book, It’s Never Just About Money: Making Peace with Your Past to Change Your Financial Future, explores the experiences, beliefs and patterns that shape our financial choices.
